Common questions

How do you play?
A friend walks up and asks for pizza, pasta or gelato. Tap that kitchen, then follow Snorfle's hints. A pointing hand shows each move: tap, rub or stir. When the dish is done it is served by itself.
My child tapped the wrong thing.
That's fine. A wrong tap only wobbles. There is no timer and no way to lose, and every dish earns stars.
What if we pick a different kitchen from the order?
The friend changes their mind and asks for something from that kitchen instead.
What is the sticker book?
Tap the book at the top of the shop front. Each dish adds a sticker, and there are 13 to collect. The Italian words your child has heard are listed there too.
There's no sound.
Turn the volume up, and check that the silent switch is off (or Silent Mode is off in Control Centre).
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. There are no ads, no purchases and no links out of the app.
Does it need the internet?
No. It works anywhere, and it doesn't collect anything. See the privacy policy.
